How to Join

New players welcome, any time of the season.

You don't need to wait for the season to start, and you don't need your own equipment. Here's how to get on the green.

You don't need equipment, experience, or a uniform to try your first end. Phone our coach Dave Vincent on 021 070 1862 — he'll set you up with a bowl and a friendly face to show you the ropes.

1. Call our coach

Phone Dave Vincent on 021 070 1862 to arrange a chat and a time to come down for a catch-up.

2. Come have a go

Visit the green, meet some members, and try a few ends with a loaned bowl — no pressure, no commitment.

3. Sign up

If you like what you find, we'll get you set up as a member. Ask about membership fees and the current season's fixtures when you visit.
